# Abstract

**Status-Relational-Entropy Dynamics (SRE-Dynamics)** is a complete physical-theory system that takes information as its ontology. It is compiled by the author from previously published manuscripts, selecting those with solid theoretical foundations and relatively objective validation data.

The theory originated on 24 April 2026, from an initial insight while pondering why electric fields generate magnetic fields: could the fundamental ontology of this world be rooted in information? Over the following months more than sixty documents were produced, most of which have been archived on Zenodo; their chronological record preserves the full evolutionary history of this theory, from its earliest conceptions and heuristic sketches up to logical closure.

The central deduction is that the universe and everything within it — including time, space, light, matter and all kinds of force-fields — emerge spontaneously from the ordered, iterative growth of a binary self-organising network, i.e. a dimension-free status-relation graph.

The book is organised into five parts. Part I, *Fundamental Axioms*, defines the interchange relationship between information-ontology and classical-physical ontology; the two ontologies can be combined and mutually transformed in engineering practice. Part II, *Emergence of All Things*, presents falsifiable inferences for the emergence processes of the material world. Part III, *Mathematical-Physical Support*, builds a complete operator system on binary self-organising networks, yielding efficient and concise graph algorithms that support local parallel graph computation on very-large-scale graph networks; beyond serving the present dynamical framework, this operator suite can also be applied independently to other engineering scenarios. Part IV, *Applications and Hypotheses*, gathers topics that are independent of the world-information-ontology premise and can directly deliver commercial value — signal processing, AI models, graph computing and brain science — alongside subjects that remain purely hypothetical at the present stage, such as instantaneous bidirectional optical communication, inclusive-benefit medical care, next-generation semiconductor design, anti-gravity and life science. Part V provides the supplementary programs and datasets.

A defining feature of this work is the full open-source release of **Operators 1–19**: their complete mathematical specifications, algebraic derivations and simulation code constitute the main body of Parts III and V. This includes the two electronic-degrees-of-freedom extension operators 11 and 12 introduced in September 2026, and the spectral-equation-solving and charge-symbol-channel operators 13–19. The entire pipeline is released under an open-source licence for reproduction, audit and secondary development.

All theoretical materials are archived in the Zenodo open-access repository and sorted by public-release timestamp. Versions v1.5.x and earlier are historical heuristic drafts, intended solely for version traceability and not recommended as formal citation sources; the formal citation baseline is the **SRE-v1.6 Axiom Suite** (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22077475). All related materials for this book, together with the reproduction programs and datasets accompanying each chapter, are also available in the author's GitHub repository: <https://github.com/yuelucn/Status-Relational-Entropy-SRE-Dynamics-The-Book-of-the-Void>. An AI-assisted Tencent intelligent-document space is additionally provided for supplementary reading; the SRE document library formerly hosted in Google Gemini Notebook is no longer maintained and serves only as a historical archive.

Scientific intuition arises out of the void, and returns to the void.

# 摘要

**状态-关系熵动力学（SRE-Dynamics）** 是一套以信息作为本体的完整物理理论体系，由作者从已公开文稿中筛选理论基础扎实、验证数据相对客观的单篇文稿汇编整理而成。

本理论萌生于 2026 年 4 月 24 日——在思索电为何能够产生磁场时，产生了一个最初的灵感：这个世界的本体，会不会建立在信息之上？此后数月累计产出六十余份文档，其中大部分已上传至 Zenodo 完成归档留存；其发布时序完整保留了本理论从早期构想、启发式草图直至逻辑闭环的全部演进历史。

全书的核心推论是：宇宙以及其中万事万物——包括时间、空间、光、物质与各类力场——均是一套二元自组织网络（无维度状态-关系图）持续有序迭代、不断生长之后自发涌现出来的产物。

全书划分为五个部分。第一部分「基础公理」定义了信息本体与经典物理本体之间的互换关系，两种本体在工程实践中可以相互组合、彼此转换。第二部分「万物涌现」给出基础物质世界涌现过程的可证伪推论。第三部分「数理支撑」基于二元自组织网络构建一套完备算子体系，实现高效简洁的图算法，支撑超大规模图网络下的局部图并行计算；这套算子体系除服务于本动力学理论之外，也可以独立应用于其他工程场景。第四部分「应用与假说」汇集独立于世界信息本体论预设、可直接落地产生商业价值的方向——信号处理、AI 模型、图计算、脑科学——以及目前完全处于假说猜想阶段的议题，如光瞬时双向通讯、普惠医疗、下一代半导体设计、反重力与生命科学。第五部分为配套程序与数据。

本书的一大特征是**算子 1–19 的全部开源**：其完整数学规范、代数推导与仿真代码构成第三部分与第五部分的主体内容，包括 2026 年 9 月新增的 11、12 号电子自由度扩展算子，以及 13–19 号谱形方程求解与电荷符号通道算子；整套流水线以开源许可发布，供复现、审计与二次开发。

全部理论资料均归档于 Zenodo 开源数据仓库，并按公开发布时间排序；其中 v1.5.x 及更早版本属历史启发式草稿，仅用于版本溯源，不建议作为正式引用依据，正式引用基准为 **SRE-v1.6 公理套件**（DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22077475）。本书全部相关资料、各章复现程序与数据集，均可在作者的 GitHub 仓库获取：<https://github.com/yuelucn/Status-Relational-Entropy-SRE-Dynamics-The-Book-of-the-Void>。此外，另提供支持 AI 辅助查阅的腾讯智能文档空间用于补充阅读；原托管于 Google Gemini Notebook 的 SRE 文档库已不再维护，仅作历史存档。
