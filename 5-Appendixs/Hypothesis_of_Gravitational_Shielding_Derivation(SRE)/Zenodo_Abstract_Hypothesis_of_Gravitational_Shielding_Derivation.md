## English

Status-Relational Entropy (SRE) Dynamics treats gravity not as a fundamental field but as a statistical thermodynamic aftereffect of local information-dissipation gradients: mass is identified with closed causal loops in a regularized causal information network, and the gravitational acceleration $a_\mathrm{SRE}$ emerges from the network's dissipation–compensation structure. This document proposes, separately and explicitly, a **hypothesis**—gravitational shielding—defines what it does and does not mean, and derives its quantitative criteria, execution mechanism, and falsifiable predictions inside the SRE axiomatic system. We deliberately avoid the phrase "anti-gravity scheme": shielding does not negate a fundamental force, and the terminological convention is fixed to "gravitational shielding", whose natural consequence is anti-gravity/weightlessness rather than a sign flip of gravity or the introduction of negative mass.

The derivation separates the network dependence of $a_\mathrm{SRE}$ into three quantities: a main amplitude $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}\cdot\mathcal{W}_{ij}$; a chiral phase term $\Gamma_\mathrm{chiral}$, the only sign-reversible term; and a uniform term $-\gamma c_\mathrm{eff}^2/4$, a global term that cannot be shielded locally. The topological stiffness weight $\mathcal{W}_{ij}$ is a strictly decreasing function of the local measurement entropy $\sigma_z$, while the dissipation tensor $\hat{\mathcal{D}}_{ij}$ is strictly increasing in $\sigma_z$; injecting local measurement entropy therefore both suppresses the amplitude and shifts the operating point of the compensation operator. This yields an exact, dimensionless shielding factor
$$\Xi=\frac{1+\ln(1+\sigma_z)}{1+\ln(1+\sigma_z')}<1$$
together with its inverse design equation, showing that the relative weakening $1-\Xi$ is independent of $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$, $r$, and $z$. Weight reduction is logarithmic in the injected entropy: $\Xi=0.98$ (about 2%) requires only a ×3 increase of $\sigma_z$, whereas complete weightlessness ($\Xi\to0$) requires $\sigma_z\to\infty$ and is unreachable. Two further geometric/phase channels are analyzed honestly: the compensation operator is non-monotonic with period $1/\alpha_0$ and a first maximum at $\hat{\mathcal{D}}_0^{*}=1/(2\alpha_0)$, but under realistic parameters $\hat{\mathcal{D}}\approx18\text{–}27$ lies in a strong-oscillation regime whose envelope mean is pinned at $1/(2\alpha_0)$, so it is mathematically valid yet engineering-fragile and not a clean knob; and the chiral term can reverse sign to give net repulsion under a strict phase condition $\mu^{*}=(4n-1)/(4\alpha_{0,\mathrm{dynamic}})$, with an order ratio to the Newtonian term of $\mathcal{R}\approx6.8\times10^{-4}/\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$, so only if $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}\lesssim8\times10^{-4}$ does chiral repulsion become comparable to or dominant over the Newtonian attraction.

The only engineerable actuation mechanism is Op2 maximum-entropy pruning, which sets a channel's spin to the multiplicative identity $+1$, eliminating its phase contribution without breaking graph connectivity. Because the interior of a mass closed loop is non-addressable and non-writable (the read-only barrier) and existing edges cannot be rewritten (read-only inheritance), shielding is necessarily a boundary-layer, incremental operation: Op1 adds frontier nodes, Op2 gates redundant edges, Operator 10 protects bridge edges ($\lambda_2>0$) and screens candidates by effective topological impedance, and only low-impedance (non-bridge) edges are allowed to go dormant. This yields a structural bound on the shieldable channel fraction,
$$\frac{N_\text{gate}}{|E_\partial|}\le 1-\frac{2}{\langle k\rangle},$$
showing that the ceiling is set by boundary topological redundancy, not by energy alone: low-connectivity, near-bridge boundaries are structurally unshieldable. Three intensity tiers are defined—A weight reduction, B shielded weightlessness, and C topological decoupling—and Tier C is shown to be mechanically excluded by the read-only barrier and read-only inheritance taken together. Hard boundaries are stated explicitly: the uniform term is unshieldable; the compensation operator acts as a self-repair mechanism that demands continuous power; and no zero-input steady-state weightlessness exists, so the hypothesis is not a perpetual-motion claim.

As a phenomenological test, the hypothesis reverse-explains the Podkletnov–Nieminen result (about 2% weight reduction above a rotating YBCO disk, stronger for a bilayer, and the later "impulse gravity generator") as a boundary-effect coherent saturation rather than an interior effect, and predicts the co-occurrence of: an abrupt disappearance of the effect at the superconducting Tc, a dependence on rotation direction (chirality), a product law for multiple layers, independence of the shielded object's composition and density, a slight increase of the disk's own inertial mass, and geometric directionality along the rotation axis. It also explains why independent groups fail to reproduce the effect: boundary redundancy is an exogenous local quantity that differs across samples; coherent saturation is a critical phase transition that fails avalanche-like off the critical point; evolution is path-dependent and non-ergodic under read-only inheritance; frontier expansion injects uncontrollable random differences; self-repair requires continuous power and a correct sampling window; the chiral phase is signed and exogenous; and a 2% effect is easily mechanically short-circuited by buoyancy, thermal drift, or support. A falsifiability boundary is stated so that the "hard to reproduce" argument cannot become unfalsifiable: if an experiment simultaneously satisfies these conditions and still measures no effect, Tier A weight reduction is falsified.

The result is presented as a hypothesis, not a confirmed claim. The decisive unresolved parameter is the dimensionless $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$, which appears in the framework but is never calibrated; whether true net anti-gravity is reachable therefore reduces to its experimental order of magnitude, to be fixed by a future Newtonian-limit measurement. The document suite—including the system paper, application development, scientific hypotheses, the complete algebraic derivations of Operators 1–19, and the simulation code—is fully open-sourced on Zenodo.

## 中文

状态‑关系熵（SRE）动力学不把引力当作基本场，而视为局部信息耗散梯度带来的统计热力学后效：质量被认同为正则化因果信息网络中的闭合因果环，引力加速度 $a_\mathrm{SRE}$ 由网络的耗散–补偿结构涌现。本文在论文之外单独、明确地提出一个**假说**——引力屏蔽，界定其含义与边界，并在 SRE 公理体系内推导其定量判据、执行机构与可证伪预言。我们刻意回避"反重力方案"这一说法：屏蔽并不抵消某种基本力，术语一律统一为"引力屏蔽"，其自然结果是反重力/失重，而非翻转引力符号或引入负质量。

推导把 $a_\mathrm{SRE}$ 对网络状态的依赖分解为三项：主振幅 $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}\cdot\mathcal{W}_{ij}$；手性相位项 $\Gamma_\mathrm{chiral}$（唯一可反号项）；以及均匀项 $-\gamma c_\mathrm{eff}^2/4$（全局项，不可局域屏蔽）。拓扑刚度权重 $\mathcal{W}_{ij}$ 是局域测量熵 $\sigma_z$ 的严格递减函数，而耗散张量 $\hat{\mathcal{D}}_{ij}$ 是 $\sigma_z$ 的严格递增函数；因此"注入局域测量熵"这一动作既压低振幅，又移动补偿算子的工作点。由此得到精确、无量纲的屏蔽因子
$$\Xi=\frac{1+\ln(1+\sigma_z)}{1+\ln(1+\sigma_z')}<1$$
及其反解设计方程，并证明相对引力削弱量 $1-\Xi$ 与 $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$、$r$、$z$ 均无关。减重随注入熵呈对数关系：$\Xi=0.98$（约 2%）仅需 $\sigma_z$ 增大 ×3，而完全失重（$\Xi\to0$）需 $\sigma_z\to\infty$，不可达。另两条通道被诚实评估：补偿算子以周期 $1/\alpha_0$ 非单调、首个极大在 $\hat{\mathcal{D}}_0^{*}=1/(2\alpha_0)$，但现实参数下 $\hat{\mathcal{D}}\approx18\text{–}27$ 处于强振荡区，其包络均值被钉在 $1/(2\alpha_0)$，故数学上成立、工程上脆弱，不构成干净的旋钮；手性项在严格相位条件 $\mu^{*}=(4n-1)/(4\alpha_{0,\mathrm{dynamic}})$ 下可反号给出净排斥，其与牛顿项的量级比为 $\mathcal{R}\approx6.8\times10^{-4}/\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$，故仅当 $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}\lesssim8\times10^{-4}$ 时手性排斥才与牛顿吸引同量级甚至主导。

唯一可工程化的执行机构是 Op2 极大熵剪枝：把通道自旋置为乘法单位元 $+1$，在不破坏图连通性的前提下消去其相位贡献。由于质量闭环内部不可寻址、不可写（只读屏障），且既有边不可改写（只读继承），屏蔽必然是边界层、增量式操作：Op1 接入新增前沿节点，Op2 选通冗余边，算子 10 保护桥接边（$\lambda_2>0$）并以有效拓扑阻抗筛选候选，只允许低阻抗（非桥接）边休眠。由此得到可屏蔽通道比例的结构上界
$$\frac{N_\text{gate}}{|E_\partial|}\le 1-\frac{2}{\langle k\rangle},$$
表明上界由边界拓扑冗余度而非仅由能量决定：低连通、近桥的边界结构性不可屏蔽。给出三档强度——A 减重、B 屏蔽失重、C 拓扑解耦——并证明档 C 被只读屏障与只读继承**双重排除**。硬边界被明确列出：均匀项不可屏蔽；补偿算子即自修复机制，需要持续供能；不存在零输入的稳态失重，因此本假说不是永动机式主张。

作为唯象检验，本假说把 Podkletnov–Nieminen 结果（旋转 YBCO 盘上方约 2% 减重、双层更强、以及其后的"脉冲引力发生器"）反向解释为**边界效应**的相干饱和，而非内部效应，并预言：效应在超导临界温度 Tc 处突然消失、依赖旋转方向（手性）、多层呈乘积律、被屏蔽物的减重与自身成分/密度无关、超导盘自身惯性质量略增、以及沿转轴的几何方向性。同时解释了为何独立团队难以复现：边界冗余度是外生局域量、各家不同；相干饱和是相变临界，离临界点即雪崩式失效；演化在只读继承下路径依赖、非遍历；前沿扩张自带不可控随机差异注入；自修复需要持续供能与正确采样时窗；手性相位带符号且外生；而 2% 的效应极易被浮力、热漂移或支撑机械短路。为使"难以复现"不至于沦为不可证伪的托词，本文给出可证伪边界：若某实验**同时**满足上述条件仍测不到效应，则档 A 减重被证伪。

本结果以**假说**而非已确证主张的形式给出。决定性的未定参数是无量纲 $\alpha_\mathrm{scale}$——它在框架中出现但从未被标定；"真正的净反重力是否可达"因此归结为其可实验判定的量级，有待未来的牛顿极限测量锚定。文档套件（系统论文、应用开发、科学假说、算子 1–19 的完整代数推导及仿真代码）已在 Zenodo 完全开源。
