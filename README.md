## Abstract & Preface (English)
Status‑Relational‑Entropy Dynamics (SRE‑Dynamics) is a complete physical‑theory system that takes information as its ontology. It is compiled by the author from previously published manuscripts, selecting those with solid theoretical foundations and relatively objective validation data.

The theory originated on 24 April 2026, from an initial insight while pondering why electric fields generate magnetic fields: could the fundamental ontology of this world be rooted in information? Over the following months more than sixty documents were produced, most of which have been archived on Zenodo; their chronological record preserves the full evolutionary history of this theory, from its earliest conceptions and heuristic sketches up to logical closure.

The central deduction is that the universe and everything within it — including time, space, light, matter and all kinds of force‑fields — emerge spontaneously from the ordered, iterative growth of a binary self‑organising network, i.e. a dimension‑free status‑relation graph.

The book is organised into five parts:
1. **Part I Fundamental Axioms**: Defines interchange between information‑ontology and classical‑physical ontology; dual ontologies can be combined and transformed in engineering practice.
2. **Part II Emergence of All Things**: Falsifiable inferences for emergence processes of material world.
3. **Part III Mathematical‑Physical Support**: Construct complete operator system for binary self‑organising networks, enabling efficient graph‑algorithms for large‑scale local‑parallel graph computation. Operator suite can be applied independently to other engineering scenarios.
4. **Part IV Applications and Hypotheses**:
    - Practical directions (independent of information‑ontology premise): signal processing, AI models, graph‑computing, brain science (commercial‑value feasible).
    - Pure‑hypothesis subjects: instantaneous bidirectional optical communication, inclusive‑benefit medical care, next‑generation semiconductor design, anti‑gravity, life science.
5. **Part V Supplementary Programs and Datasets**.

A defining feature of this work is the full open‑source release of **Operators 1–19**:
Their complete mathematical specifications, algebraic derivations and simulation code constitute the main body of Parts III and V.
Includes extension operators `11,12` (electronic degrees‑of‑freedom, Sep 2026), and operators `13‑19` (spectral‑equation solving & charge‑symbol‑channel).
The entire pipeline is released under open‑source licence for reproduction, audit and secondary‑development.

> Scientific intuition arises out of the void, and returns to the void.

## 作者序（中文）
**状态‑关系熵动力学（SRE‑Dynamics）** 是一套以信息作为本体的完整物理理论体系，由作者从已公开文稿中筛选理论基础扎实、验证数据相对客观的单篇文稿汇编整理而成。

本理论萌生于 2026 年 4 月 24 日——在思索电为何能够产生磁场时，产生了一个最初的灵感：这个世界的本体，会不会建立在信息之上？此后数月累计产出六十余份文档，其中大部分已上传至 Zenodo 完成归档留存；其发布时序完整保留了本理论从早期构想、启发式草图直至逻辑闭环的全部演进历史。

全书的核心推论是：宇宙以及其中万事万物——包括时间、空间、光、物质与各类力场——均是一套二元自组织网络（无维度状态‑关系图）持续有序迭代、不断生长之后自发涌现出来的产物。

全书划分为五个部分：
1. **第一部分 基础公理**：定义了信息本体与经典物理本体之间的互换关系，两种本体在工程实践中可以相互组合、彼此转换。
2. **第二部分 万物涌现**：给出基础物质世界涌现过程的可证伪推论。
3. **第三部分 数理支撑**：基于二元自组织网络构建一套完备算子体系，实现高效简洁的图算法，支撑超大规模图网络下的局部图并行计算；这套算子体系除服务于本动力学理论之外，也可以独立应用于其他工程场景。
4. **第四部分 应用与假说**
    - 可落地方向（不依赖信息本体论预设）：信号处理、AI 模型、图计算、脑科学，具备商业落地潜力；
    - 假说猜想议题：光瞬时双向通讯、普惠医疗、下一代半导体设计、反重力、生命科学。
5. **第五部分 配套程序与数据**。

本书一大特征：**算子1‑19完整开源**
完整数学规范、代数推导与仿真代码构成第三、五部分主体；包含2026‑09新增11、12号电子自由度扩展算子，以及13‑19号谱形方程求解、电荷符号通道算子。整套流水线开源发布，支持复现、审计、二次开发。
